ANGEL — Streetwear reseller storefront demo

OPEN: Extract the ZIP, then double-click index.html to open in Chrome. Live Server is optional.

FILES: index.html, style.css, script.js, assets/*.svg.

FEATURES: 12 illustrative product listings; search; brand/category filters; price sort; product details and sizes; cart with quantity and removal; persistent cart (localStorage); checkout form and demo order confirmation; responsive design.

IMPORTANT: This is a fictional demo store. Brands are named solely as sample resale catalog labels. It is not affiliated with Supreme, BAPE, or Chrome Hearts. Product images are original stylized SVG illustrations, not actual branded product photos; names, prices, and inventory are placeholders. Do not advertise the sample items as authentic or available. Replace with photos of verified inventory you own or are authorized to use, with accurate pricing and product descriptions, before any real-world use. Checkout does not process payments, submit orders, or collect information on a server.

CUSTOMIZATION: Edit the products array at the top of script.js; change each image path to assets/your-photo.jpg after copying your photos into assets.
